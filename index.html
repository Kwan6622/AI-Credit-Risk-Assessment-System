System Scope & Multi-Phase Roadmap

Version 1.0 focuses exclusively on personal consumer loans. Secured lending and corporate financing modules belong to future development phases.

Version 1.0 — Active

Personal Consumer Loans

Small personal retail loans for household purchases, shopping, and everyday consumer spending. Features benchmarked against the Home Credit Default dataset with probability calibration and SHAP explainability.

Version 2.0 — Planned

Secured Lending

Dedicated mortgage and auto-finance modules utilizing collateral valuations, property-specific risk factors, and domain-appropriate underwriting datasets.

Version 3.0 — Concept

Corporate Credit Analysis

Commercial balance sheet distress modeling, corporate financial debt servicing covenants, and business cash-flow solvency assessments.

TINEX CreditRisk AI System

A practical machine learning engineering portfolio project combining supervised classification benchmarks, model calibration, and explainability for consumer credit risk.
